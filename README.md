# PROG101-ASSIGNMENT1-BINTA-HULLYMATU-KAMARA-905006519
Design and Development of a Community Drug Abuse Awareness and Referral System Using Computational Thinking

## Background of the Problem
Drug abuse is a public health and social concern because the harmful use of substances can affect a person’s health, judgement, relationships, education, work and safety. The effects may extend beyond the individual to parents, guardians, friends, schools, health workers and the wider community. Young people can be particularly vulnerable when they face peer pressure, misinformation, stress, limited access to support or difficulty discussing substance-related concerns.
In Sierra Leone, communities need clear, respectful and practical ways to share prevention information and connect people with appropriate support. This report does not claim a particular national prevalence rate because the assignment brief does not provide a statistical dataset. Instead, it analyses the community challenge at a logical-design level and proposes a system that can be adapted after consultation with local health and social-service providers.

## Description of the Problem
The problem selected is the lack of a simple, trusted and organised channel through which community members can access basic drug-abuse prevention information, raise a concern safely and obtain guidance on where to seek help. Information may be scattered across schools, families, health facilities and community organisations. A person may fear judgement, stigma or exposure, while a family member may be unsure what information to provide or which service to contact.
The proposed solution addresses an information and referral process only. It does not identify a person as a drug user, investigate alleged offences, store unnecessary sensitive details, recommend medication or make clinical decisions. In an emergency or where someone may be in immediate danger, the system must direct the user to local emergency services or the nearest appropriate health facility rather than attempting to manage the emergency itself.

## Pseudocode
```
Function Main
    Declare Integer Choice

    Output "COMMUNITY DRUG ABUSE AWARENESS AND REFERRAL SYSTEM"
    Output "For emergencies, contact local emergency services or visit a health facility."
    Output "Enter your choice:"
    Input choice
    If choice = 1
        Output "Drug Abuse Awareness Information"
    Else
        If choice = 2
            Output "Support and Referral"
        End
        If choice = 3
            Output "View Support Contacts"
        End
        If Choice= 4
            Output "Goodbye"
        Else
            Output "Invalid choice. Please enter a number from 1 to 4."
        End
    End
End
```
